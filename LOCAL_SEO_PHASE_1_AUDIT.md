# Noble Hardwoods Local SEO — Phase 1 Evidence and Publishing Audit

Audit date: August 4, 2026
Scope: 27 current city/state location pages plus four strategic areas from the original SEO plan

## Phase 1 outcome

The location-page system has enough aggregate evidence to support a strong local SEO program, but not enough city-attributed evidence to treat all 27 pages as finished local authority pages.

The best strategy is to concentrate the next implementation phase on eight Tier 1 markets. Together, those markets represent 236 of the 315 projects with a known location, or 74.9% of the current evidence base.

Current evidence summary:

- 315 closed deals have a known city/state in the supplied report.
- 27 city/state pages currently exist.
- Eight Tier 1 markets account for 236 known-location projects.
- Eleven Tier 2 markets account for 67 known-location projects.
- Eight Tier 3 markets account for 12 known-location projects.
- Three current location pages have a matching project case-study record: Overland Park, Lenexa, and Lawrence.
- Briarcliff has a project case-study record but is absent from the current location dataset and closed-deal report.
- No stored review currently has a verified city attribution.
- Fifteen named-home/apartment photographs are available but have no city or project mapping in the repository.

## Evidence rules for later phases

1. A project count is quantitative proof, but it is not a substitute for a real local case study.
2. A photograph must not be described as being from a city until its project and location are confirmed.
3. A review must not be presented as a local testimonial until the reviewer’s city is confirmed.
4. Mission, Kansas and Mission Hills, Kansas must remain separate evidence records.
5. Kansas City, Missouri and Kansas City, Kansas must remain separate pages and reporting records.
6. Briarcliff, Brookside, and Waldo should be treated as Kansas City neighborhoods, not separate municipalities.
7. Local copy should be based on actual project patterns or crew experience, not generic landmarks or demographic filler.
8. Operations should confirm that every “closed deal” in the source report represents a completed project before the terminology becomes a permanent published claim.

## Indexation decisions

The audit recommends the following publishing sequence:

1. Enrich the eight Tier 1 pages and treat them as the first local SEO release wave.
2. Upgrade Tier 2 pages only as attributable proof becomes available.
3. Keep Tier 3 cities in aggregate service-area coverage until they can provide a useful standalone experience.
4. Create Briarcliff only after its neighborhood structure and evidence are confirmed.
5. Hold Mission Hills, Brookside, and Waldo until project evidence can be mapped.

If all 27 current pages are already publicly indexable, Phase 2 should prepare the technical controls needed to align the sitemap and indexation strategy with these decisions. This audit does not change live indexation by itself.
